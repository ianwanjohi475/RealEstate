Our story

Born out of frustration, built on proof

Our founders spent three weekends driving across Nairobi to view "available" homes — half of which didn't exist, were already taken, or came with quiet extra fees. It was exhausting, and it was normal.

So we built the agency we wished existed, and gave every home a single, honest number — the Esto TrustScore — that summarises four independent checks before you ever view.

Today Esto is a trusted name for buyers, renters, sellers and diaspora investors who want certainty in a market that rarely offers it.

How TrustScore works

Four checks, one honest number

Every listing is scored 0–100 as the average of four independent verifications.

Title & ownership

Physical inspection

Price fairness

The asking price is benchmarked against live area data.

Agent record
