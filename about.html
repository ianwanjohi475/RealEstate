Our story

Born out of frustration, built on proof

Our founders spent three weekends driving across Nairobi to view "available" homes — half of which didn't exist, were already taken, or came with quiet extra fees no one mentioned online. It was exhausting, and it was normal.

So we built the agency we wished existed: one where a listing only goes live after a human has stood inside it, confirmed the title, and photographed exactly what you'll get. No stock photos. No bait. No games.

Today Nyumba is a trusted name for buyers, renters, sellers and diaspora investors who want certainty in a market that rarely offers it.
